# ⚔️ Last Knight
### Fight. Upgrade. Survive.

**Last Knight** is a top-down 2D medieval survival game where you fight endless waves of enemies, collect coins, choose powerful upgrades, and grow stronger with every run.
There is no end to this game, only stronger players!

# 🎮 Getting Started

When you first enter Last Knight, you are prompted to the Login/Sign Up screen, where you can save your progress as you play the game. From there, you are brought to the main menu, which is where you can access the different parts of the game, view your progression, customize your player, and begin a new run.

The main menu provides access to:

- Enter Arena
- Quests
- Achievements
- Leaderboards
- Stats
- Skills
- Shop
- Settings

Your goal is to use these features to continually improve your knight and survive longer during each run.

---

# 🗡️ Starting A Run

Clicking on "Enter Arena" begins a new run.

You are placed into the medieval battlefield and immediately begin fighting enemies.

During a run, your main objectives are to:

1. Defeat enemies
2. Collect coins
3. Choose upgrades
4. Survive increasingly difficult waves
5. Have fun

Every run can play differently because the upgrades and enemies you encounter can vary.

# 👹 Enemy Waves

Enemies continuously enter the battlefield and attempt to defeat your knight. As you survive more waves, the challenge icnreases. This means that simply surviving the first few waves is not enough: you need to continually improve your character through upgrades. Your **wave count** represents how far you have progressed during your current run. The goal is to survive for as many waves as possible.

# 🃏 Choosing Upgrades

One of the main features of Last Knight is choosing your upgrades. At the start of every wave, you are presented with multiple randomly selected upgrades. You choose one of the available options and immediately apply it to your character. Because the choices are randomized, you cannot rely on the same upgrade paht every time. This allows different runs to end up in different ways depending on the upgrades you receive and the choices you make.

---

# 💰 Coins

Coins are the game's main permanent currency. Coins can be collected by defeating enemies, finishing runs, completing quests, and receiving play time rewards. They can be used to:
- Improve your skill tree
- Purchase skins
- Contribute toward your overall progression 
- Help you climb the leaderboard

This gives players a reason to keep playing even after a run ends.

---

# 🌳 Skill Tree

The **Skills** menu contains the game's permanent progression system. Coins can be spent on skill upgrades that improve your overall stats. Unlike temporary upgrades obtained during a run, skill tree upgrades are applied to every run. This means that the work you put into previous runs can continue helping you during future runs.

---

# 🎨 Skins

The **Shop** allows players to spend their coins on skins. These provide players with ways to customize the appearance of their knight for runs.

---

# 🏆 Leaderboards

The **Leaderboard** allows players to compare their performance with other players. Your performance can be tracked through the game's global leaderboard system, giving you an additional goal beyond simply completing a single run. The objective is to survive longer, improve your performance, and climb higher on the leaderboard. 

---

# 📊 Stats

The **Stats** section allows players to view information about their progress and performance. This gives players a way to look back at their runs and see how they have progressed throughout the game.

---

# 📜 Quests

The **Quests** menu gives players additional objectives to complete outside of simply surviving waves. Quests provide smaller goals to work toward while playing and give players additional reasons to return to the game.

---

# 🏅 Achievements

The **Achievements** system gives players long-term goals to accomplish. Instead of only focusing on surviving the current run, achievements encourage players to explore different parts of the game and reach specific milestones.

---

# 🔐 Accounts & Saved Progress

Last Knight includes an account system powered by **Supabase**. Players can create an account and log in to the game. Player information and progression can then be saved, allowing important progress to persist between sessions. This feature also supports the game's global leaderboard functionality.

---

# 🔁 The Full Gameplay Loop

Last Knight is designed around a simple but repeatable progression cycle:
**Play > Fight > Choose Upgrades > Survive > Earn Coins > Improve Player Stats > Play Again** Each run helps you work toward becoming stronger, while the increasing difficulty gives you a reaosn to keep pushing your limits.
